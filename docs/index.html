Small Print

How this is measured

Nothing here is an opinion about code quality. It is dates and counters, with jokes on top.

Status

Days since the last push to any branch: up to 45 is alive, up to 180 coughing, up to 365 flatlined, more is buried. Archived by the owner is embalmed.

Hosted tools

A site that answers is judged the same way, by the day its page was last edited: a date the page declares or prints, its Last-Modified header, or the newest entry in its sitemap. No answer means buried. A robot being turned away (403, 429) counts as an answer.

Pulse score

0 to 100. 40 points are recency of the last push, 40 points are stars on a log scale (1,000 stars is full marks), 20 points are how long the project was worked on, capped at a year.

Who gets in

Repositories created since 2023 whose name, description or topics mention captures (pcap, Wireshark, tshark) and AI (LLM, GPT, MCP, agent). Found by scripts/sweep.mjs every Monday. A few are admitted by hand.

"Vibe-coded"

A genre label, not an accusation. We cannot tell how a repository was written and do not claim to. Several entries are careful work that simply stopped, and some are finished.

Causes of death

Invented, every one. They are drawn from a short list by the repository's name. Epitaphs are generated from dates, or written by hand where a tool earned it.

Wrongly buried?

Push a commit and the next sweep moves you. For anything else, open an issue. Removal requests from authors are honoured without discussion.

Disclosure

This list is kept by the maker of PacketSafari, which is listed and judged by the same rules. Pull requests to bury it are accepted when the dates say so.
