How this is measured

Nothing here is an opinion about code quality. It is dates and counters.

Status

Days since the last push to any branch: up to 45 is alive, up to 180 coughing, up to 365 flatlined, more is buried. Archived by the owner is embalmed.

Pulse score

0 to 100. 40 points are recency of the last push, 40 points are stars on a log scale (1,000 stars is full marks), 20 points are how long the project was worked on, capped at a year.

Who gets in

Repositories created since 2023 whose name, description or topics mention captures (pcap, Wireshark, tshark) and AI (LLM, GPT, MCP, agent). Found by scripts/sweep.mjs, weekly.

"Vibe-coded"

A genre label, not an accusation. We cannot tell how a repository was written and do not claim to. Several entries are careful work that simply stopped.

Wrongly buried?

Push a commit and the next sweep moves you. For anything else, open an issue. Removal requests from authors are honoured without discussion.
